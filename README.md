# Chinar Clash — The Great Kashmiri Kursi Fight

A 3D political-satire fighting game set in Kashmir. Pick your neta, fight for the kursi.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages site for this repo (link in About) — no install, runs in the browser.

## Roster
Omar Skiwala · Mehbooba Madam · Farooq Dada · Sajad Solo · Altaf Tycoon · Azad Sahab · Engineer Rashid — and a locked boss, **Sher-e-Kashmir**, who unlocks after a 3-match win streak.

## Stages
Dal Lake · Lal Chowk · Gulmarg — all procedural Three.js scenes.

## Controls
Move A/D · Jump W · Crouch S · Block hold away · Punch J · Kick K · Grab I · Super L (full SHAKTI meter) · Special forward+J/K · Sweep S+K · Pause Esc. Touch buttons appear on mobile.

## Tech
Three.js (CDN) + vanilla JS. No backend, no accounts — progress lives in your browser's localStorage. Best of 3 rounds, 60-second timer, SHAKTI super meter, CPU on 3 difficulties.

## Satire notice
Political satire and parody. All characters, dialogues and headlines are fictional, exaggerated caricatures for humour and social commentary. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any person, party or government body. Cartoon violence — do not imitate.
